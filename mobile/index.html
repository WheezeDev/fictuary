Your stories live only on this phone. A backup is one file with everything in it: stories, tags, notes and where you are. Keep it in iCloud Drive, Google Drive or OneDrive.

How to add a story

  1. Open the story on AO3.
  2. Tap Download, then HTML. It saves to your Downloads.
  3. Come back here, tap Add story, and pick the file.

For new chapters, do the same again. Your tags, notes and place are kept.
